## Prompt

Create one original seamless square tileable game texture for a Doom-compatible reinforcement-learning environment. Asset type: orthographic industrial ceiling material viewed as a flat plane. Subject: a dark gunmetal ceiling grid with recessed square service panels, narrow structural ribs, small restrained amber and cyan indicator pixels, subtle grime, and no lamps or bright glare. Composition: a uniform repeating planar material with no focal point; opposite left/right and top/bottom edges must join seamlessly. Style: crisp low-resolution 1990s first-person-shooter pixel art, hard pixel clusters, limited readable palette, designed to survive nearest-neighbor downscaling to exactly 64x64 pixels. Lighting: flat neutral material lighting, no directional cast shadows, no perspective, and no overall gradient. Exclude characters, creatures, weapons, scenery, openings, vents with visible depth, text, logos, watermarks, UI, borders, frames, and recognizable copyrighted designs. Make the texture fully opaque.
